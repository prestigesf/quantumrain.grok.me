# What you want vs what it does

## You want

You sit at QuantumRain. You drop a law pack. Your engine grades it. You see whether it applies and whether the score moved. You can do that alone.

## It does now (App.v2)

- **Drop pack** walks every compiled DeadlineSF pack (14), not a short demo list.
- **Drop file** loads your YAML.
- **Intercept** runs `engine.decide` in the page (DeadlineSF engine.mjs).
- **Evidence ON** marks every control SATISFIED so the outcome can leave HUMAN_REVIEW.
- Receipts list on the right. Strength ledger is the old 41.5→100 chain. New runs at head 100 show Δ 0.

## Still true

The 100 is the ledger head. Intercept does not invent a higher number. The grade (APPLIES / HUMAN_REVIEW / ALLOW) is the live result.
