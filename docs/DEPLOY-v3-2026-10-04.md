## Publishing
quantumrain.grok.me is served by Grok App Builder (`grok-project-id 01a07abc-50b8-7f10-835c-f77d810d9582`). Git pushes do not deploy it. `.github/workflows/pages.yml` only checks that files exist, and `CNAME` dates from the old Pages setup. To go live: open that project in Grok (Build), bring in this branch's files, then **Publish app** to `quantumrain.grok.me`.
